How to play

You're an ordinary Clevelander with $8, 50 health, and a long walk to the lakefront. Fight a little. Eat something. Learn a move. Go back stronger.

Lost after exploring?

The Street Directory is your way back.

  1. After a street encounter, choose Return to Street Directory in the action choices. You can also tap DIRECTORY at the bottom left of the scene artwork.
  2. If buttons are disabled, let the story finish typing. Click the story text, or press Space or Enter, to finish the current line. For faster play, turn on Settings (top bar) → Instant text.
  3. If Explore Street started a fight, defeat the alien to receive the bounty automatically and return to the directory, or choose Flee and then Return to Street Directory. You cannot visit shops during a fight.
  4. Inside a shop, choose Leave to return. The directory lists only the shops and landmark on your current street, plus nearby travel options.

Use Settings in the top bar for this guide, text speed, and the Paper / Neon theme switch. Close the window to return to play.

01 / Get your bearings

Your first few fights

  1. Choose Start Surviving, then Start Adventure.
  2. Choose Hunt an Alien from your first street for a guaranteed encounter after a 2.5-second search. Watch the search meter fill before combat begins. You can also find aliens through Explore Street. After a win, your cash and street progress update automatically.
  3. Visit the Coffee Shop when hurt. Read the effects under each item before buying.
  4. Save for a training tape at the Record Store. Purchased moves appear in your next fight.

Explore Street can lead to supplies, locals, news, shops, or a fight. Explore Safely appears on cleared streets, where no aliens remain. After an event, Keep Exploring continues wandering.

02 / Spend to survive

Build your street fighter

  • Food and drinks: restore health. Eligible items also grant a first-taste bonus once per shop/item: maximum health, punch, kick, or accuracy.
  • Training tapes: buy Grappling ($18) and Counterattack ($20) at the Ontario Record Store. Find Spinning Kick ($32) at the Ontario Pawn Shop, Lake Effect Kick ($24) at the Euclid Arcade, and Ironworks Punch ($28) at Old River Army Surplus.
  • Conditioning: permanent defense, bought in three stages at the Superior Drugstore, Superior Thrift Store, and Broadway St. Stanislaus. Each stage requires the previous one.
  • Gear: weapons last three swings, including misses. Armor wears out after a set number of hits.

Permanent means for this run: upgrades survive defeat and Continue. Starting a new run resets them. Revisit earlier shops by walking back to their street.

03 / Read your opponent

Make every turn count

Tackle is reliable but weaker; Punch balances accuracy and damage; Kick hits harder but misses more often. Special moves may be limited to once per fight. Guard halves incoming damage; the Counterattack tape lets you strike back after blocking a hit.

Blue / QuickAccurate strikes. Watch your health.

Green / HeavyBig hits, lower accuracy. Guard softens the blow.

Grey / PsychicIgnores worn armor. Guard and conditioning still help.

Red / RageGets stronger as rounds pass. Finish the fight quickly.

04 / Reach Lake Erie

Ten districts. Thirty-six wins.

Enemies get gradually tougher as you reach later streets. Streets 1–3 take two regular victories and one boss victory. Streets 4–9 take three regular victories and one boss victory. The final street has two guards and the Mothership Commander. The counter above the story shows your progress, and the directory changes to Challenge District Boss when the boss is next.

Clearing a boss opens the road ahead and grants +10 maximum health and a full heal. You stay on the cleared street until you choose the adjacent street marked Active →. Defeat the two guards in the final district, then beat the Mothership Commander to save Cleveland.

Travel one street at a time. Adjacent destinations are marked Cleared or Active. Cleared streets have no battles; traveling does not reset wins or heal you. A locked road appears in the Field Log until you defeat the district boss.

Fleeing does not count as a victory. Bounties depend on the district and whether the enemy is a boss; taking extra turns does not earn more money.

05 / Live to fight again

Save, quit, come back

PROGRESS SAVED confirms a local checkpoint. Use Continue on the title screen to resume. During combat, reloading returns to the last completed turn; outside combat, Continue returns to the directory of the street you were visiting. Existing saves and purchased moves carry over.

After defeat, Recover at shelter restores full health and keeps your upgrades and district wins. You lose 25% of your cash, rounded up. Recovery is free at $0.

Use the same browser and site address. Saves do not sync between devices or between the live site and a local copy. Clearing browser data removes them. Starting a new run asks before replacing your save.
